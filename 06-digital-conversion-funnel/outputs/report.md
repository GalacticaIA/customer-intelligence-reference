> Synthetic, deterministic data. This is a reproducible analytical artifact, not a client result.

| Step | Reached | From previous | Lost at transition |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Product visit | 10,000 | — | — |
| Application started | 5,830 | 58.3% | 4,170 |
| Application submitted | 4,224 | 72.5% | 1,606 |

Overall conversion: **15.3%**.

The largest transition loss occurs before **Application started**. A sensitivity scenario that improves that transition by 5% absolute yields approximately **131 additional first transactions**, holding every downstream rate constant.

That last number is a transparent what-if, not a forecast: it answers which step deserves an experiment before anyone assigns revenue to it.
